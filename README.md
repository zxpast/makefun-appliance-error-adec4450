> Beep. Your food is hot on the edges and cold in the middle. I did my part. Physics did the rest.

The fault is real — standing waves heat food unevenly, hot at the edges and cold in the middle. The machine speaks in its own flat, put-upon voice, and the twist lands on the last words, with nothing explained.
